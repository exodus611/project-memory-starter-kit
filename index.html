The ZIP contains exactly one file: START_HERE.md. The block and the NOTES.md template are both inside it — nothing else to download.

How it works (3 lines)

  1. Once: create a private repo, copy the NOTES.md template, fill in Goal, Current state, Repository map.
  2. Every session: paste one block from START_HERE together with your NOTES.md.
  3. The AI does the rest: opens by reporting state and next task, drafts checkpoints in the middle, and writes the closing update at the end. You just say yes / no / commit.

Sometimes asked

Is it one block or eight? One block. The older kit had several per-stage files; they are now in reference/ for the curious. You never need them.

Do I maintain the note myself? No. After setup, the AI drafts every update; you review and approve.
