# Checkpoint message

Use this after a material event and before context compaction, a tool or model switch, or a transfer.

```text
Prepare a draft checkpoint for the repository.

Separate verified facts from assumptions. Include:
- the event and date;
- what changed or was tried;
- the result and evidence;
- the decision and reason, if any;
- failed approaches and why not to retry;
- the files that contain the detail;
- the effect on current state;
- the next action;
- the proposed updates to NOTES.md and any detailed project files.

Show me the draft and the relevant diff. Do not commit until I have reviewed
it, unless I have explicitly authorized write-back for this workflow.
```

A checkpoint is a current, inspectable repository update. It is not a claim that the AI remembers everything in the chat.
